# Phase 3 — feature ideas for the next update

These are proposals only; they are not included in the Phase 1–2 stability patch.

## Recommended features

### 1. Innerwear Style Finder
A guided discovery flow that lets a visitor choose a style (Brazilian, cheeky, high-leg, thong, boyshort, brief, mesh, lace, strappy or bodysuit), coverage preference, fabric feel and colour. It should return matching campaign references and clearly label them as inspiration until real product/catalogue data exists.

### 2. Build Your Set
A visual outfit builder to combine coordinating top, bottom and optional robe/garter pieces. Save several combinations locally, compare colour/fabric directions and generate a shareable look card. No checkout or stock claim without a verified catalogue.

### 3. 3D Garment Viewer
A phone-friendly product viewer with front/back/side angles, pinch-to-zoom and optional slow rotation for garment-only 3D models. Use non-explicit mannequin or garment renders; keep the viewer lightweight and provide a static-image fallback for low-end phones.

### 4. Fit & Comfort Finder
A short, privacy-first guide for rise, waist position, coverage, stretch and fabric preference. It should avoid claiming exact sizing without the brand's verified measurements, explain that sizing differs by product and avoid storing body measurements by default.

### 5. Accessory-ready Design Edit
Explore fashion-led garment construction concepts such as decorative removable rings, adjustable straps, detachable trims and accessory-compatible styling. Keep product information garment-focused, with material, comfort, wash-care and safe-use notes; no device is bundled or implied.

### 6. Wishlist & Compare
Save looks into named lists such as Everyday, Lace, After Dark and Gift Ideas. Compare fabric, cut, coverage and campaign image side-by-side. Keep lists on the device until an authenticated backend is intentionally introduced.

### 7. Collection Drops & Editorial Calendar
Seasonal launch pages with a countdown, campaign story, gallery, matching art direction and a reminder button. Reminder storage should be local unless the user later chooses a backend.

## Suggested order for Phase 3

1. Innerwear Style Finder + Build Your Set.
2. Wishlist & Compare.
3. 3D Garment Viewer after optimized 3D assets are available.
4. Fit & Comfort Finder once an approved measurement chart is supplied.
5. Collection Drops and local reminders.

## Inputs needed before product-commerce features

A real catalogue should provide product IDs, names, sizes, brand measurement charts, material/care details, prices, stock, product photos, variant photos and product-page URLs. Until then, campaign images and reference concepts must not be presented as purchasable stock.
