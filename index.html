FIT, COMFORT & CARE

A better fit starts with good measurements.

Use the size guide supplied with the specific garment. The current source repositories contain campaign and editorial images, but do not provide a verified, universal size chart for every style.

  1. 01 · Band / underbustMeasure around the ribcage where the band sits. Keep the tape level and comfortably snug.
  2. 02 · BustMeasure around the fullest part without compressing the body. Follow the garment maker's instructions.
  3. 03 · Waist & hipFor briefs or shaping styles, take the garment's requested waist and hip measurements.
  4. 04 · Check the fitLook for secure support without pinching, digging, slipping or restricted movement.

Before choosing a size

  • Read the specific product's measurement chart and fabric notes.
  • Check stretch, lining, cup construction and adjustability.
  • Choose comfort and freedom of movement over a label number.
  • For a fit concern, use the brand's verified support/contact channel when one is published.

No product price, stock, fabric composition or S/M/L conversion is invented here. Those details must be added from a verified product catalogue before checkout can be enabled.
